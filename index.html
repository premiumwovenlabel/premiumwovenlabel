Give your brand a professional finish

A woven label is made on a loom. Instead of printing your logo onto a surface, coloured threads are woven together so the design becomes part of the fabric — your logo, brand name, text and detail, reproduced in thread.

It’s a small piece of a garment, but it carries a lot. Here’s why brands use them:

  • Strong brand identityYour name sits inside every piece you make.
  • Professional appearanceThe finish customers already associate with established labels.
  • Durable brandingThe design is woven in, not applied on top.
  • Premium presentationA detail people notice when they try a garment on.
  • Design flexibilitySize, shape, fold and thread colours are chosen per order.

Woven labels vs printed labels

Both have their place. The difference comes down to how the design gets onto the material.

Woven labels

Made on a loom, in thread

  • The design is created by weaving coloured threads together on a loom.
  • Holds fine detail in thread, so logos and small text stay legible.
  • A raised, fabric texture you can feel — the finish associated with retail clothing.
  • Commonly used for neck labels, brand tabs and hang tags on clothing and fashion.

Printed labels

Printed with ink, onto a base material

  • Ink is printed onto a base material such as satin or cotton tape.
  • Can reproduce photographic detail and gradients that thread cannot.
  • A flat, smooth surface — the design sits on top of the material.
  • Often used for care instructions and long text where wide detail matters.

Common questions

Short, factual answers to what people ask us most.

What are woven labels?

Woven labels are small pieces of fabric made on a loom, where your logo, brand name or text is created by weaving coloured threads together. The design is part of the material itself rather than ink sitting on a surface.

What are woven labels used for?

They are used to brand clothing and products — neck labels carrying a brand name, side-seam tabs, size and care labels, hang tags on the outside of a garment, and monograms or crests on school uniforms.

What is the minimum order quantity?

For clothing and fashion brands, the minimum is 1,000 pieces. For schools ordering custom monograms or crests, the minimum is 200 pieces.

How long does an order take?

Production takes 7–10 days once the design, size and quantity are confirmed, for delivery across Pakistan. International orders are arranged separately and confirmed with you before dispatch.

Can I add my own logo?

Yes. Send us your logo over WhatsApp or email and we will tell you how it translates into thread, including any detail that needs simplifying at label size.

Can I customise the size?

Yes. Labels are made to the size you need rather than fixed sizes. Tell us the garment and where the label sits, and we will suggest a size that fits and stays readable.
